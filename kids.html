COVID Stories for Kids

This page explains COVID-19 stories in a simple and safe way. It is made to help younger readers understand what people experienced.

What was COVID-19?

COVID-19 was a virus that spread around the world. Many people had to stay home, wear masks, and be careful to protect others.

Why do people share stories?

People share their stories so we can understand what life was like and remember what everyone went through together.
