COVID Stories (Simple Version)

A gentle, easy explanation of what COVID-19 was and why people share stories about it.

What was COVID-19?

COVID-19 was a virus that spread around the world. It changed how people worked, learned, and spent time with others.

Why do people share stories?

People share stories so we can understand history through real experiences and remember what life was like.

What did people feel?

Many people felt confused, lonely, or worried — but also found ways to stay connected and help each other.
